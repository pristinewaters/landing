About Us

Guardians of the river's silence

Pristine Waters was born at the southernmost tip of the continent, where the wind never rests and the rivers run clear and cold from glacier to sea. We are anglers and naturalists first. Outfitters second.

Every journey is designed around you: the water you want to fish, the pace you want to keep, and the quiet you came south to find. What we offer is simple: expert guiding, honest gear, and a table set beside the river when the fishing is done.

  • Bilingual expert guides
  • Small, private groups
  • Catch & release ethic

The Program

We arrange everything. You fish.

From windswept steppe to ancient lenga forest, your guide decides where, when and how you fish. We handle everything else, so all you have to do is fish.

Bilingual Guiding

Local guides who read water, wind and wildlife, and explain it all in your language.

Door-to-River Transport

Hotel or port pick-up in a 4×4, with the flexibility to adjust for weather and last-minute changes.

Premium Gear

Rods, waders, boots and a curated fly box, all included. Travel light, we've got you covered.

Gourmet River Lunch

Local cheese, charcuterie, fruit and Chilean wine, served riverside between runs.

A Day on the Water

How a day with us unfolds

07:30

Pickup & Briefing

We collect you from your hotel or the port and brief you over coffee.

09:00

On the River

Walk & wade, run by run, guided through water only locals know.

13:00

Riverside Lunch

We break for a full gourmet spread, set on the bank, wine included.

16:30

Last Light & Release

The evening rise, careful releases, and the ride back as the wind finally settles.

A day as it often goes. Every itinerary is shaped around each angler.
