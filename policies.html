FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous fly-fishing experience?

No. Beginners and experienced anglers are welcome.

Is all equipment included?

Yes. We can provide all the necessary equipment if required.

Are fishing licences included?

Yes.

Are meals and beverages included?

Yes, unless otherwise specified.

Are park entrance fees included?

No.

Can non-anglers join the trip?

Please contact us to discuss the available options.

Can trips be customized?

Absolutely. We can tailor an experience according to your schedule, interests and experience level.

Do you practice catch and release?

Yes. All Pristine Waters fishing experiences operate under a strict catch-and-release policy.

What happens if the weather changes?

Patagonian weather changes quickly. Our guides will always select the safest and most suitable option according to the prevailing conditions.

Can you help us with transportation and logistics?

Yes. We are always happy to help our guests make the most of their time in Patagonia.

Still have a question?

We're here to help before, during and after your trip.
